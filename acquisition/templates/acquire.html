Hallmark Reader: Image Acquisition and Recognition (companion application)

This application reads the visible characters of a hallmark inscription and estimates how reliable the reading is. It does NOT verify gold purity, authenticity or BIS certification. The laptop webcam is a software demonstration of the acquisition workflow; no wrist-worn device was built.

Why a laptop webcam is usually not enough

Hallmark characters are only about 1 to 3 mm high. A typical laptop webcam is fixed-focus at roughly 40 cm or more, so a hallmark held at that distance covers only a few pixels per character and cannot be read reliably. Use the upload option with a close-up/macro photograph, or a USB macro camera / USB microscope. The proposed wearable uses an OV2640 macro-optics camera on an ESP32-S3 board (not built here); see the paper.

An uploaded image goes through exactly the same validation and recognition pipeline as a webcam capture.
